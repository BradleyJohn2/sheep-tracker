✨ What's new in v7

NEW
IndexedDB storage engine
Data now stored in IndexedDB instead of localStorage. No 5 MB limit — handles years of records on any device.
NEW
12-month hot store + archive
Weight records from the last 12 months show on each sheep card inline. Older records move to archive automatically — tap "View full history" on any card to see everything.
FIX
Safe storage quota guard
If storage ever fills up you get a clear warning modal with a direct link to Settings — no more silent data loss.
FIX
Record ID collision fix
Record IDs now use crypto.randomUUID() instead of timestamps — prevents duplicate IDs when saving rapidly or syncing between devices.
INFO
Backup includes archive
Backup files now include all archived records — your complete history is always in the download regardless of age.
INFO
Storage health indicator
Settings tab now shows a live storage health bar so you can see how much data you have at a glance.
